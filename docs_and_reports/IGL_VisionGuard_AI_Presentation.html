SLIDE 02 AI DETECTION PIPELINE

Two-Pass Mobile Phone Misuse Detection Engine

Pass 1: Full-Frame Inference

Runs COCO-SSD on raw camera video at low confidence cutoff (0.15) to extract all person detections and candidate objects.

Pass 2: Upper-Body ROI Crop & Zoom

Crops person head, ears, face, chest & hands region (+20% margins), upscales to 360x360 offscreen canvas, and re-runs COCO-SSD to detect small phones near ears/face.

SLIDE 03 PPE HELMET INSPECTION

Per-Person Dedicated ML Helmet Verification

1. Head Crop Tensor

Crops top 25% head region of tracked person box & resizes to 224x224 tensor.

2. Conservative Default

Strictly defaults to hasHelmet: false if custom model files are unsupplied or score < 0.65.

3. Temporal Memory

Requires 3 consecutive persistence frames + 3 miss grace frames to prevent flickering.

SLIDE 05 GEOFENCING & NEAR-MISS

Dangerous Zone Boundary Box & Distance Radar

Dangerous Zone Boundary Box (Geofence)

Polygon boundaries drawn on camera screens (e.g. High-Voltage Transformer Area) trigger instant alerts & siren when workers step inside using Ray-casting point-in-polygon math.

Worker-Vehicle Distance Radar

Live distance measurement between forklifts/machinery and workers on foot with dynamic slider movement (0.2m DANGER to 3.0m SAFE) and perimeter risk badges.
